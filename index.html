( A family farm near Akranes )

Móar, a family farm withchickensand a dog calledTáta

Móar is a family-run farm in Innri Akraneshreppur, 301 Akranes. The log cottages stand along the gravel track and the red timber guesthouse on the lawn. There are chickens on the farm, and Táta is the farm dog.

( The night sky )

aclear view of thenight sky from the lawn

From the lawn at Móar there is a clear view of the night sky. In the dark months the northern lights can show over the mountain on a clear night. Nobody can promise them: the weather and the sun decide.
